# Source documents

The primary sources the VAL backend's architecture is built from
(see [docs/val-backend.md](../val-backend.md) for how each maps onto the
code), with provenance and redistribution notes.

**Only the public-domain DOT assessment ships in the repository.** The
other two documents are not cleared for redistribution: they are
`.gitignore`'d, kept as local working copies in this directory, and were
purged from the git history. Obtain them from their archives as noted
below and drop them in here — nothing in the build depends on them.

## `VAL_Assessment.pdf`

G. Anagnostopoulos, *Interim Assessment of the VAL Automated Guideway
Transit System*, U.S. DOT / Urban Mass Transportation Administration,
report **UMTA-MA-06-0069-81-3** (DOT-TSC-UMTA-81-34), November 1981.

Provenance: U.S. DOT National Transportation Library (ROSAP), digitized
by the Internet Archive; originally distributed to the public through
NTIS.

**Redistribution: safe.** A work of the United States federal
government (prepared by the DOT Transportation Systems Center), not
subject to copyright under 17 U.S.C. § 105, and expressly released for
public sale/distribution.

## `These_Verhille.pdf`

Jean-Noël Verhille, *Représentation énergétique macroscopique du métro
VAL 206 et structures de commande déduites par inversion*, doctoral
thesis, ENSAM / L2EP Lille, 2007. Deposited on HAL (the French open
archive).

**Redistribution: not cleared — not shipped.** HAL open access grants
free reading and download from the archive, but the author retains
copyright and no Creative Commons licence is attached to the deposit —
mirroring the PDF in a public repository is reproduction beyond what
the archive's terms grant. Download it from HAL (search hal.science for
the title) into this directory; the file is ignored by git.

## `SiemensTransportationSystemsInc_03152007.pdf`

City of Chicago, Department of Aviation / Department of Procurement
Services: sole-source application and Technical Services Agreement
package for Siemens Transportation Systems, Inc. (O'Hare Airport
Transit System), specification B2-92955-03, February–March 2007.

**Redistribution: low practical risk, not formally licensed — not
shipped.** A municipal public procurement record (state/local
government works are not automatically public domain the way federal
works are), consisting almost entirely of forms, budget tables and
equipment lists with minimal creative content. Kept as a local working
copy only; the board nomenclature it contributed is quoted where used
(`VALArchitecture.swift`, `docs/val-backend.md`).
